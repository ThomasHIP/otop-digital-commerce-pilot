Why now / เหตุผลที่ควรทำตอนนี้

โอกาสเชิงยุทธศาสตร์
CDD มีเครือข่ายจังหวัดและอำเภอที่เข้าถึงผู้ประกอบการ OTOP ได้ทั่วประเทศ ขณะที่ Shopee programme ทำให้ผู้ประกอบการมีแรงจูงใจเข้าสู่ตลาดออนไลน์มากขึ้น จากค่าธรรมเนียมพิเศษและการสนับสนุนการเปิดร้าน
Strategic opportunity
CDD already has a national field network through provincial and district offices. The Shopee programme creates a strong entry point for OTOP merchants to become online sellers, with marketplace benefits and seller activation needs.

Training Model / รูปแบบอบรม

Recommended format

1 Day / 6 Hours practical workshop, supported by pre-onboarding and post-training online clinic. Suitable for large batches similar to professional government/BOI-style training.

Capacity

300–500 merchants / batch with 1 lead trainer, 4–6 technical trainers and 10–15 floor coaches.

09:00–10:30

Opportunity + Seller Setup

Programme briefing, account, phone verification, shop profile.

10:45–12:00

Content Gathering

Product story, title, photo, description, price, stock, SKU.

13:00–14:30

Product Listing

Upload first products and check listing quality.

14:45–15:45

Shipping + Payment

Bank, payout, delivery, packing and order handling.

15:45–16:30

GTM + Completion

Promo basics, QR/LINE/Facebook, completion status and next steps.

Project Timeline / แผนดำเนินงาน

1Week 1: CDD approval, target provinces, recruitment kit
2Week 2: Merchant registration, readiness screening, batch allocation
3Week 3–4: First training batches and live activation tracking
4Day +7 / +14: Online clinic and completion verification
5Month 2–3: Scale to more provinces and prepare FastPage/E-Commerce upgrade

Operating principle

Do not start with 98,000 merchants at once. Start with a controlled provincial pilot, prove completion rate, then expand in batches while CDD HQ monitors every merchant status.


สถานะติดตาม: Registered → Qualified → Attended → Store Created → Products Uploaded → Store Live → FastPage Candidate → E-Commerce Candidate.

Success KPIs / ตัวชี้วัดความสำเร็จ

Stage 0 KPIs

Registered merchants, qualified merchants, attendance rate, Shopee store created, first 3–5 products uploaded, shipping/payment readiness, store-live status.

Stage 1 KPIs

FastPage generated, 5-language content complete, Google Business matched, merchant confirmation completed, public page published.

Stage 2 KPIs

Product catalogue ready, checkout tested, QR PromptPay/card readiness, merchant receives order, fulfilment process confirmed.

CDD Dashboard

Province, district, merchant status, training batch, issue category, completion result, follow-up status and next action.
